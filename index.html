Our Story

A little café with a
big heart

Tucked away at 1 Bank Place in Kilmarnock town centre, The Coffee Press is the kind of café that keeps you coming back. Whether you're popping in for a flat white, a hearty brunch, or a slice of something sweet — you'll always leave smiling.

We take pride in great-quality ingredients, seasonal specials, and a space where you can truly relax. Families, solo readers, and groups alike are all welcome here.
